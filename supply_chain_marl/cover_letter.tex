\textbf{CCD-MAPPO: A Coupling Coordination Degree Signal\\
for Cooperative Multi-Agent Supply Chain Resilience}
\end{center}

\textbf{Motivation and scope.}
Global supply chains face an underappreciated structural challenge: firms differ not only
in \emph{how much} data they exploit, but in whether they invest broadly across many
data sources (breadth) or deeply in a few (depth).  This breadth--depth heterogeneity,
captured by the Coupling Coordination Degree (CCD) from coupling-coordination theory,
shapes disruption recovery speed in ways that aggregate digitalization indices obscure.
No prior multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework embeds this structural
dimension into the state space or reward signal.  Our paper closes that gap.

\textbf{Contributions.}
We propose CCD-MAPPO, a MAPPO algorithm augmented by the CCD signal, with three
principled theoretical contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Representational efficiency} (Proposition~1): no symmetric linear
    feature $\alpha B + \beta D$ can distinguish the balance non-linearity that CCD
    encodes; CCD's canonical C/T factorization is analytically irreducible.
  \item \textbf{Provable safety} (Proposition~2): a residual-policy construction
    anchored on the $(s,S)$ heuristic guarantees worst-case performance at the
    classical baseline and preserves B2B information privacy at execution time.
  \item \textbf{Linear scaling} (Proposition~3): a graph neural network critic with
    $O(|\mathcal{E}|)$ parameter complexity---vs.\ $O(|\mathcal{N}|^2)$ for MLP
    critics---enables deployment on industrial-scale supply networks.
\end{enumerate}
Against five baselines over four disruption scenarios (100 rollouts each), CCD-MAPPO
achieves a 4.1\% improvement over vanilla MAPPO, 12.4\% over QMIX, and 23.7\% over
the $(s,S)$ heuristic, all confirmed at $p<0.05$ by Wilcoxon rank-sum tests.

\textbf{Fit with \textit{Annals of Operations Research}.}
The paper combines stochastic dynamic optimisation, supply chain management, and
MARL---an intersection central to AOR's interdisciplinary scope.  The safety guarantee
and closed-form scaling result provide the analytical rigour expected of the journal,
while the four-scenario experiment grounds the contributions in operations practice.
